# Food Nomads

A small group of former colleagues who meet every two weeks for lunch in and around Basel, and never go back to the same place twice.

## Language

**Member**:
A person in the group with access to the app, known by a unique display name. All Members have equal rights.
_Avoid_: User, account, admin

**Deactivated Member**:
A former Member who can no longer log in. Their past Votes, comments and Photos remain.
_Avoid_: Deleted user, banned

**Venue**:
A place the group could go for lunch: restaurant, beer garden, food truck, etc.
_Avoid_: Restaurant, place, location

**Visited Venue**:
A Venue at which at least one Lunch has taken place. A Visited Venue can never be chosen again.
_Avoid_: Used, done, closed

**Successor Venue**:
A new Venue that replaces a Visited Venue at the same place after a substantial change (new chef, new owner, new concept). It links to its predecessor and records the reason. It starts unvisited.
_Avoid_: Reopened venue, reset

**Lunch**:
One meetup of the group on a specific date at one Venue.
_Avoid_: Visit, event, meeting, outing

**Planned Lunch**:
A Lunch with a date (no time) that has not happened yet. There is at most one at a time: any Member can plan one when there is none. It starts without a Venue; any Member picks one of its Candidate Venues, and can change the pick while it is planned. Any Member can move it to another date; that clears its RSVPs. It stays planned after its date until a Member marks it held or cancels it.
_Avoid_: Upcoming, scheduled, draft

**Held Lunch**:
A Lunch that took place. Any Member marks a Planned Lunch as held, on or after its date and only once it has a Venue; nothing happens automatically. A Lunch from before the app can be recorded directly as held, with its date and Venue. Holding a Lunch turns its Venue into a Visited Venue.
_Avoid_: Past, completed, done

**Cancelled Lunch**:
A Planned Lunch that any Member called off. It stays visible with its comments, and leaves its Venue and Candidate Venues unvisited.

**Candidate Venue**:
An unvisited Venue a Member put up for a Planned Lunch. Any Member can add one, and remove one that isn't picked, together with its Votes. A new Planned Lunch has none.
_Avoid_: Proposal, option, suggestion

**RSVP**:
A Member's answer, in or out, about attending a Planned Lunch. A Member who hasn't answered has no RSVP. Moving the Lunch to another date clears all RSVPs. Conditions ("only if we stay near X") go into a Lunch Comment.
_Avoid_: Registration, attendance

**Vote**:
A Member's up or down on a Candidate Venue of a Planned Lunch. A Member has at most one Vote per Candidate Venue and can change or withdraw it. Votes guide the pick; they don't decide it.
_Avoid_: Like, upvote, star

**Venue Comment**:
A Member's remark on a Venue itself ("buffet only at noon"), independent of any Lunch.

**Lunch Comment**:
A Member's remark on a Lunch, before it (arranging) or after it (the write-up). A Lunch's comments read as a chat, oldest first.
_Avoid_: Chat message, review

**Photo**:
A picture a Member took at a Lunch, attached to that Lunch.
_Avoid_: Image, upload, attachment

**Invite**:
A single-use, time-limited link one Member gives to a new person so they can join as a Member.
_Avoid_: Registration, signup

**Open Invite**:
An Invite that can still be redeemed: it has not been redeemed, revoked or expired. Any Member can see and revoke Open Invites.
_Avoid_: Pending invite, active invite

**Bootstrap Invite**:
The one-time Invite that exists only while there are no Members, used to let the very first Member in.

**Recovery Link**:
A single-use link one Member issues for another Member who lost access, so they can register a new passkey. Using it revokes all their old passkeys.
_Avoid_: Password reset
