# Successor Venue instead of reopening a Visited Venue

The group's rule is "never the same place twice, unless something substantial changed (new chef, new owner)". We model the exception as a new **Successor Venue** that links to its predecessor and records the reason. We do not "reopen" the Visited Venue. This keeps the invariant strict: a Venue has at most one Held Lunch. A Venue's Votes, comments and Photos always refer to the same incarnation of the place.
